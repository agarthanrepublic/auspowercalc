Protection coordination

The shaded band is the device's real time–current curve (from manufacturer time–current data). The sloped line is the selected cable's adiabatic thermal-withstand limit t = (k·S / I)². For coordination the device must clear below the cable curve at the prospective fault current — so the breaker trips before the cable is damaged. Cable, size, fault level and clearing come from the Single circuit tab.

Voltage drop: PU = (Site VD − FSC VD) / ΣLₙ; mains and sub-mains target PU × length (capped by each board's own max VD), final sub-circuits target the FSC allowance. Fault level: three-phase prospective fault from the transformer (or supply fault level) through each cable at 20 °C; mains and sub-mains must withstand the fault at their supply end for the clearing time (k²S² ≥ I²t). Earth fault-loop: maximum length per AS/NZS 3000 Appendix B, L = 0.8·U₀·Sₚₕ·Sₚₑ / (ρ·Iₐ·(Sₚₕ+Sₚₑ)); the earth conductor starts at AS/NZS 3000 Table 5.1 and is increased until the length complies.
