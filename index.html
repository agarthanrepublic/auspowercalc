Protection coordination

The shaded band is the device's real time–current curve (decoded from PowerCad's exported data). The sloped line is the selected cable's adiabatic thermal-withstand limit t = (k·S / I)². For coordination the device must clear below the cable curve at the prospective fault current — so the breaker trips before the cable is damaged. Cable, size, fault level and clearing come from the Single circuit tab.

How this maps to PowerCad-5 & the .LIB format
QuickCable is an independent, clean-room implementation of the AS/NZS 3008.1 cable-sizing method, mirroring PowerCad-5's Cable Selection and single-line workflow. Cable ratings are decoded from PowerCad .LIB files for interoperability; the sizing engine and diagram are original. Results are indicative — verify against the current standard and manufacturer data. Not affiliated with PowerCad Software Pty Ltd.
